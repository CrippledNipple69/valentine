So Ananya,

I want to ask you something big and serious —
something serious at par with your favourite momos,
kebabs, chicken bhaat, your favourite kurtis,
and honestly… everything that you love.

So listen, and think carefully, my grace 🤍

I’ve wanted to ask you this confidently —
on the night we met, with all the fairytale things around us.

Ever since then, I feel like I should’ve asked you boldly,
like a prince — and not fumbled in front of
your world-alluring beauty.

So I’ll do that now.

Will you be my Valentine,
the ever-bewitching
Princess of the Chelmsford? 👑🤍

(A “No” will be hard to catch for me,
but please do try once.)

🎵 Me & You
